Pokémon Gym Leader Directory

Explore the rich history and powerful Gym Leaders of the Kanto and Johto regions.

Region 01

Kanto Region

Kanto is the foundational region of the Pokémon world, characterized by its dense cities, tech hubs like Saffron City, and diverse natural terrains. From the rocky paths of Pewter City to the fiery volcanoes of Cinnabar Island, Kanto established the formal Gym League challenge under the oversight of Indigo Plateau.

Region 02

Johto Region

Located directly west of Kanto, Johto is deeply steeped in tradition, myth, and ancient architecture. Known for landmarks like the Brass Tower and Sprout Tower, Johto shares its Pokémon League with Kanto at Indigo Plateau while preserving its unique culture and heritage.
